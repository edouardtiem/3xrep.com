# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Business-to-business salespeople and managers preparing the next action on a real deal.

## Product Purpose

Build a deal strategy from the context people bring to their assistant. README.md remains the source of product truth.

## Operating Context

Claude Cowork or ChatGPT Work on a computer. The assistant maintains authorized local files and can use connected sources. The 3xrep server judges supplied evidence.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Deterministic evidence checks, gaps, methods, strategies and conditional replies. No autonomous prospect contact, invented quotes, win probabilities or automatic CRM writes. Public scenarios are synthetic. Beta enrollment and selection follow docs/beta/founding-20.md.

## Evidence on Hand

Existing brain and synthetic strategy tests in src/lib/brain. No customer story or search-volume claim is authorized.

## Product Principles

Evidence before assumptions. One commercial logic shared by product and public scenarios. Useful content before signup.
